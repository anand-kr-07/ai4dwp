Title: Outlook closes unexpectedly - quick help you can do now
Version: v 1.0
Date: 07/08/2026
Status : Draft

If Outlook keeps closing, do not worry. Your email, calendar, and contacts are safe in your company account. These steps are safe to do by yourself.

1. Close Outlook fully.
What to do: If Outlook is open, close it and wait 30 seconds.
What success looks like: Outlook is no longer on your screen.

2. Reopen Outlook.
What to do: Open Outlook from the Start menu.
What success looks like: Outlook opens and stays open for at least 2 minutes.

3. Restart your computer.
What to do: Save any open work, then restart your computer.
What success looks like: You can sign back in and open Outlook again.

4. Check your internet connection.
What to do: Make sure you are connected to your normal office or home network.
What success looks like: Internet pages open normally.

5. Try Outlook again.
What to do: Open Outlook and check one email, open Calendar, and send one short test email.
What success looks like: Outlook stays open while you do all three steps.

6. Wait a few minutes and try again.
What to do: If it closes again, wait 5 minutes, then open it once more.
What success looks like: Outlook opens and keeps working normally.

When you contact the service desk, share:
- The time it closed (latest time and any earlier times)
- What you were doing just before it closed (reading email, search, calendar, sending mail)
- Whether restart helped or not
- Whether your internet connection was working normally
- Any message shown on screen (exact words if possible)
- Your device name (if known)
- Best phone number and time to call you

Contact the service desk if Outlook still closes after you complete all steps above.
